A FEW HELPFUL ANSWERS

Good questions.
Beautiful beginnings.

Have something else in mind?
Ask us on WhatsApp ↗

What products can I inquire about?

Start with strip lashes, lash clusters, applicators, makeup brushes, blending sponges and related beauty accessories. Send a reference photo if you are looking for a specific item.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Minimum quantities depend on the item, style and packaging. Share your desired quantity so we can discuss suitable options. No single minimum applies to all products.

Can I request samples or custom packaging?

Yes, include your sample or packaging requirements in your inquiry. Availability, customization options, sample costs and lead times need to be confirmed for the specific product.

How do I get a quotation?

Send the product category, reference photos or links, quantity, packaging requirements and delivery country through WhatsApp. Pricing and shipping are confirmed after the brief is reviewed.

Can I place an order on this website?

This is a product showcase and inquiry website. Product specifications, availability, payment and delivery terms are agreed directly before an order is placed.
