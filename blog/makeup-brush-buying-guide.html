A practical makeup brush buying guide

Brush collections are easier to plan when every brush earns a place. Begin with the makeup functions your customer uses, then refine the details that shape feel, finish and retail presentation.

A product photo is only the start of a brush conversation. The function, fibre profile, ferrule, handle and packaging all affect the option you are assessing. Here is a simple way to organize the brief.

1. Choose functions before shapes

First decide what the set needs to help a customer do. A compact set for travel, a beginner kit and a professional-facing assortment will not need the same mix.

  • Face: powder, complexion, contour or finishing functions.
  • Eyes: packing, blending, detail and liner functions.
  • Finishing: a brush that supports the final step your customer values.

One brush can sometimes perform more than one job. That may be useful in a small kit, but the description should explain the intended function rather than make broad performance claims.

2. Ask about the physical details

DetailWhat to clarify
FibreAsk about the fibre type, density and feel for the specific brush option.
Brush headCompare size, shape, cut and intended product application.
FerruleConfirm finish, color and how it relates to the overall presentation.
HandleConsider length, color, material preference and logo placement.
Set presentationDiscuss pouch, carton, sleeve or other packaging requirements.

Request actual photos or samples when possible. They are more useful for judging an option than generic category imagery, especially when color or finish matters to your brand.

3. Keep the assortment clear for the buyer

A concise assortment is easier to merchandise and explain. You might begin with a core set, then add individual brushes or a more premium presentation once you understand the response from your customer.

For each selected brush, record its working name, intended use, reference image, preferred color direction and any packaging note. This becomes a practical comparison sheet when you request details.

4. Turn the plan into an inquiry

Send the number of brushes you want to consider, their intended functions, any references, your quantity range, packaging idea and destination country. Ask for product-specific information, sample availability, minimum quantities, pricing and lead-time details before deciding.
