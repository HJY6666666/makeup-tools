Beauty-tool inquiries for international buyers

If you are sourcing false lashes, makeup brushes, blending sponges or related beauty accessories from outside China, a well-prepared inquiry makes the next conversation clearer.

MAKEUP TOOLS is an English-language product showcase and inquiry website. Start a WhatsApp conversation in English or Chinese, then share the details that matter for your particular product project.

How do I start an international inquiry?

Send the product category, a few reference images or links, the quantity range you are considering, any packaging idea and your destination country. This lets the discussion focus on the actual option you need rather than a generic category.

Share firstWhy it helps
Product directionName the category and describe the look, shape, function or finish you are interested in.
ReferencesLinks or images help communicate a direction. Describe the features you like rather than requesting a copy of another brand’s product.

What can be discussed?

Begin with strip lashes, lash clusters, applicators, makeup brushes, blending sponges and related beauty accessories. Ask for actual product photos, available options, product specifications, sample information, quantities and a product-specific quotation.

Availability, minimum quantities, sample costs, packaging options, pricing, production timing, payment and shipping arrangements are confirmed for the actual product and order details. This keeps expectations clear before you make a decision.

A simple message to copy

“Hello MAKEUP TOOLS, I am an international buyer interested in [product category]. I am looking for [style, shape, material or function]. My expected quantity is [range]. I would like to discuss [samples / packaging / product details]. My destination country is [country]. Here are my references: [links or images].”
